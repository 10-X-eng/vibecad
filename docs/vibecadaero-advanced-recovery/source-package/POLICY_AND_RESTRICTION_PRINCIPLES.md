# Third-Party Information Principles

1. **Component-specific scope.** A third-party component's terms apply to that component within the scope of its own license. Do not generalize them into VibeCAD/Aero-wide restrictions.
2. **Documentation is the product mechanism.** Keep authoritative licenses and readable notices available. Do not add purpose/use detectors, commercial/military blockers, license-hash gates, compliance SBOM gates, or solver entitlement logic.
3. **One informational acknowledgement.** First Aero entry may show one notice with checkbox **“I understand.”** Store one local unversioned bit and normally never show it again.
4. **No output reclassification.** The notice does not change ownership/licensing of CAD designs or unrelated Aero results.
5. **No invented agreements.** If a third-party project does not publish a standard commercial agreement or deployment model, VibeCAD must not invent one.
6. **Engineering safeguards remain engineering safeguards.** Geometry staleness checks, solver qualification, artifact integrity, remote-upload visibility and numerical validation are technical correctness mechanisms, not license/purpose enforcement.
